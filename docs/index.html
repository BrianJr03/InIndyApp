InIndy

Discover what's going on InIndy.

InIndy is where folks share trail runs, pickup games, park picnics and whatever else going on in Indianapolis.

Free. No ads. Made in Indy.

The app

Neighborhoods

Sorted by where you live.

Posts stay in the neighborhood they belong to, so your feed looks like your part of the city.

Groups

Somewhere to keep coming back to.

Run clubs, pickup leagues, book swaps. Join a group and its posts find you.

Photos

A little less guesswork.

Photos let people see the trail, the field, the noteworthy view, before deciding to come.

Two ways to use it

If you're going

No plan required.

  • Browse what's going on near you.
  • See who else is in.
  • Show up.

If you're hosting

Easily connect with nearby folks.

  • Post the time and place.
  • Pick a neighborhood.
  • Connect with others.

Android beta

Installing the APK

  1. Open the release page using this link.
  2. Under Assets, tap the inindy-beta.apk file to download it.
  3. Your browser will ask for permission to install unknown apps. Grant it. This is a per-app Android permission, and you can revoke it afterward.
  4. Open the downloaded file and install.

Play Protect will probably warn you. Google flags anything not installed from the Play Store. Tap “More details” and then install anyway. If you would rather verify first, the SHA-256 checksum is published on the release page.

The app will not auto-update. There is no store connection, so new builds have to be downloaded manually. Watch the releases page, or the email you got with the build.

Uninstall the beta before installing the Play Store version. The two are signed with different keys, so the upgrade will fail with a signature error otherwise. Anything stored only on the device will be lost. Your account and posts are on the server and will come back when you sign in again.

We're working on releasing the Android version on the Google Play Store. In the meantime, users must download the official app from our GitHub page. Nonetheless, you'll always be able to find the latest builds here.
